Here are 3 mathematical projects that I am most proud of.

## Lifestyle Elements

I have a constant need to move physically or I would feel stagnant. This works well to help me balance a more sedentary and thoughtful lifestyle of studying or working at a desk. It all started with Parkour in 2004. It was still a rather obscure sport featuring people performing what seemed like superhuman feats of speed, strength and agility. The next 7 years were one long journey to conquer my fears (for example, of heights). As I started Ballroom Dancing in 2011, the passion for Parkour moved towards dancing. Over the next 5 years I participated in nearly 50 competitions across Canada, USA and Europe, winning some of them. Ever since I bought my first smartphone in 2013, I started snapping beautiful views. And it's so easy to find beautiful sights in Vancouver, because it's such a beautiful place. I particularly enjoy taking photos of sunsets. Some of them will be available for sale as prints soon.
